# Cinematic production target

Current acceptance target for production video generation:
- user-selectable duration; test target 40-45 seconds
- vertical 9:16
- photorealistic multi-shot storytelling
- consistent protagonist identity, wardrobe, age and facial features
- intentional cinematic camera movement and shot grammar
- natural Arabic narration with synchronized pacing
- ambient/native clip audio mixed below narration when available
- no placeholder/still-only success path
- final MP4 must pass ffprobe and motion checks

Test story: A young man finds an old camera in a rainy night market. Every photo reveals the same place decades earlier. In the final photograph, he sees an unknown figure standing behind him, but when he turns around the alley is empty.
